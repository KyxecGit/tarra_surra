Our Story

Contemporary Thai
in River North

Tarra is a contemporary Thai restaurant in Chicago’s River North, offering a modern interpretation of Thai cuisine rooted in authentic flavors and traditions.

Led by Chef Namo, the menu draws inspiration from Thailand while bringing a refined, contemporary approach to each dish. Tarra is designed as an elevated dining experience centered around thoughtful food, warm hospitality, and showcasing traditional Thai food recipes.

Culinary Direction

Expanding Traditional Thai Flavors

Brand · Kitchen · Hospitality

Tarra’s kitchen philosophy is rooted in three qualities: flavor, variety, and shareability. At its heart is balance — acidity, sweetness, saltiness, bitterness, and richness composed with intention. The dining room supports that craft: calm, elegant, and guest-first.

Culinary leadership, including Chef Namo and the wider kitchen team, works within the Tarra brand — not as a celebrity showcase, but as part of a house built to welcome more exceptional Thai talent over time.
