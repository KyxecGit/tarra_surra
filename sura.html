Thai Inspired Cocktail Lounge

The Bar

Where Night Comes Alive

Sura is the Thai-inspired cocktail lounge beneath Tarra — a sophisticated bar where the program frames Thai aromatics inside classic cocktail structure. Moody light, curated sound, and a room built for conversation as much as for the pour.

Come for a pre-dinner drink, stay for the nightcap, or make Sura the entire evening. Same address as Tarra upstairs — a separate room, a separate reservation.
